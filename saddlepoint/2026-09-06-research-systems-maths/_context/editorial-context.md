# Editorial context: Maths, Systems and Research in the Age of AI

This file preserves the intellectual frame needed to revise the essay. It is not part of the published page and is not a record of how the lecture was developed.

## Premise

AI has sharply reduced the cost of searching, explaining, calculating, coding and producing plausible answers. It has not removed the need to decide what problem is worth solving, where its boundary belongs, what evidence would count, or whether an answer should be believed.

The durable claim is:

> The working out is becoming cheap; knowing what to work out is not.

This is neither an anti-AI argument nor a claim that access is fully equal. Knowledge and first-pass specialist explanation have become much easier to reach. Reliable judgment, mentorship, compute, laboratories, instruments and time remain unequal.

## Direction

The essay proceeds in this order:

1. **Research as inquiry.** A topic becomes a research problem when its tension, feasible attack and consequence can be stated.
2. **Systems as opportunity.** Important tensions often live at boundaries, in interactions that disappear when components are studied separately.
3. **Mathematics as foundation.** Abstraction, generalization and structural recognition allow an idea to cross those boundaries.

The compressed argument is:

> A good problem needs a conflict. Conflicts sit at system boundaries. Mathematics is how we cross a boundary.

## Narrative spine

The cup of tea is the stable object. It first distinguishes an exam question, a systems question and a research question. Its demand to cool quickly and slowly demonstrates conflict. The heat equation then travels through the cup, air-conditioning systems, Kelvin's model of the Earth and diffusion models.

The recurrence is not decoration. It demonstrates the mathematical habit being advocated: apparently different situations can share a structure.

## Essential examples and their jobs

- **Davara and tumbler:** exposes the inadequacy of optimizing an object without understanding its use.
- **Personal quantile conjecture:** shows inquiry, persistence, AI-assisted checking and the scientific value of a counterexample.
- **Optimized cup:** shows that a solver can answer a poorly framed objective correctly.
- **Two air conditioners and the street:** shows that system failures live in interactions and chosen boundaries.
- **Kelvin and Perry:** shows that correct mathematics inside an incomplete boundary can yield a wrong account of the world, and that a correction may fail to travel across fields.
- **Compression and CART:** shows the historical cost of disconnected literatures.
- **Dictionary and binary search:** demonstrates abstraction and the distance between a simple idea and a reliable implementation.
- **Optimal transport:** demonstrates generalization and the recurrence of one structure across soil, images, language and generative models.
- **Diffusion models:** closes the argument by returning to the heat equation and changing an impossible inversion into a learnable question.

## Editorial guardrails

- Do not imply that AI independently chooses important questions or removes human responsibility for verification.
- Do not equate access to information with equal access to infrastructure, mentorship or judgment.
- Avoid claiming that component research is finished or that system-level problems are untouched. The defensible claim is that cross-boundary work has become easier for one person to attempt.
- Distinguish a mathematically correct answer within a model from an adequate model of the world.
- Treat personal stories as evidence of experience, not proof of a universal rule.
- Preserve qualifications when historical intention is unknown—for example, describe what the davara does without inventing why it acquired its form.
- Use “feedback loop” or “iterative error correction,” not “gradient ascent,” unless an objective and gradient are actually defined.

## Audience and desired effect

The primary reader is an undergraduate or early researcher trained to solve assigned problems but not yet taught how to frame one. The essay should replace intimidation with agency without pretending that research is easy.

The desired progression is:

1. Recognize the difference between solving and framing.
2. Learn to look for a genuine tension.
3. Examine boundaries and interactions.
4. Use mathematics to recognize transferable structure.
5. Use AI to extend reach while retaining responsibility for judgment.
